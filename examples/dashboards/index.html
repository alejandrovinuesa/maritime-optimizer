Data notice. Research and decision-support tool. Regulatory parameters come from Delegated Regulation (EU) 2023/2776, Regulation (EU) 2023/1805 (FuelEU Maritime), Directive 2003/87/EC as amended by Directive (EU) 2023/959, IMO resolutions MEPC.338(76), MEPC.353(78) and MEPC.354(78) and MARPOL Annex VI, in their version in force in 2026, and may change. Vessels are generic examples; MCR, SFOC, time-charter rates, fuel and EUA prices and canal tolls are illustrative estimates, not market quotes. Calm-water physics. Results do not replace official MRV, FuelEU or CII verification and are not legal or commercial advice.
